About Jane Duke

Jane Duke is a 21st-century Romantic composer and the founder of Revolutionary Romantic Records. Her music embodies a bold and radical vision: a return to the emotional intensity and dramatic power of classical Romanticism, fused with modern compositional and production techniques.

Deeply influenced by Marxist thought and revolutionary ideals, her work seeks to awaken strong emotions in the listener — from ecstatic hope and sublime beauty to the darkest and most disturbing depths of the human condition. Her compositions are not meant to be easy background music; they demand full attention and aim to shake the soul.

Through Revolutionary Romantic Records, Jane Duke strives to ignite a new artistic movement: one where music becomes a vehicle for profound emotional truth and radical expression in an increasingly cold and superficial world.
